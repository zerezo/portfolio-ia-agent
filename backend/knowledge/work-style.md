## Methodical
He first understands the problem and its edge cases, and then builds. For example, in this portfolio the booking form was kept out of the AI and written as plain code because dates and contact details must be exact.

## Ownership
When something fails he owns it, fixes it and learns from it, without looking for someone to blame (extreme ownership, no-blame culture).

## Teamwork
He works well in teams and naturally takes on coordinating and supporting the group, something he developed as captain of his local football team and as coordinator of activity leaders at events.

## What he learned building this portfolio
How to build AI agents with tool calling: an orchestrator that chooses between tools, retrieval over documents (RAG) and filters before the model. And how to deploy for real: separate development and production environments, Railway, Cloudflare, his own domain and CI/CD with GitHub Actions.
