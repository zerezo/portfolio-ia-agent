## Background
tags: background, biography, education, where from, trayectoria, biografia, formacion, de donde es
Alejandro is a Telecommunications Engineer from the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, based in Segovia (Spain). He works in cybersecurity at IndraMind Cybersecurity, in the Cyber Risk & Compliance area.

## Why cybersecurity
tags: why cybersecurity, GRC, risk, career change, por que ciberseguridad, riesgo, cambio
Two things took him from telecommunications to cybersecurity governance, risk and compliance (GRC). First, he likes acting as a bridge between the technical side and the business: turning networks and systems into risks and decisions that a business can understand. Second, he wants to understand risk itself: how technology fails and how an organisation is protected, not only how technology is built.

## Why AI
tags: why AI, artificial intelligence, FDE, forward deployed, clients, por que IA, inteligencia artificial, clientes
He is drawn to applied AI because he wants to build together with clients: stay close to their real problem and take the solution all the way to production, not just a prototype. He enjoys fast-paced environments where he owns the result from start to finish. This portfolio, an AI agent built and deployed on his own, is an example of that.
