## Origin
He was born in 1993 in Cervera, a small town in Lleida (Catalonia, Spain). He started riding motorbikes as a child and his younger brother Álex also became a professional rider.

## Career summary
He reached the top category of motorcycle racing (MotoGP) in 2013 and won the title in his first season. He has won world championships in 125cc, Moto2 and MotoGP.

## Comeback
A serious arm injury in 2020 kept him away from competition for a long time and needed several surgeries. He describes the recovery as the hardest and most formative period of his life.
