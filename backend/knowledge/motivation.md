## What motivates him
Working close to real problems and owning the result: understanding what a client needs, building the solution and making sure it works in production. He is motivated by the bridge between technology and business, and by understanding and reducing risk.

## Goals
In three to five years he sees himself leading a technical team that works close to clients. He has already coordinated people as football team captain for several years and as activity coordinator at children's events, and he wants to grow that into leading engineering teams.
